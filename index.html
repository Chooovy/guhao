Now · KV-cache eviction & sparse attention

Hao Gu

Making large language models smaller, faster, and able to read longer.

I'm a researcher at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), working on efficient large language models. My work keeps asking one question — how much of a model, its memory and its compute do we actually need? — and answers it with sparse attention that scales toward infinite context, KV caches that learn what to forget, mixture-of-experts models compressed by merging and decomposition, and weights quantized down to sub-1-bit.

Recently I led BTC-LLM (ACL 2026), QaRL (Findings of ACL 2026), D²-MoE, and our study of the gating mechanism of KV-cache eviction. I also contributed to HiLS-Attention, hierarchical sparse attention open-sourced by Tencent Hunyuan.
