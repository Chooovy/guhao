Now · Sparse attention @ Tencent Hunyuan

Hao Gu

Building language models that know where to look — across very long contexts.

I'm a Ph.D. student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) and a research intern at Tencent Hunyuan.

I work on the architecture and pre-training of large language models. These days I mostly think about sparse attention: how a model can decide which parts of its context deserve compute, so that attention scales toward very long contexts without giving up quality.
